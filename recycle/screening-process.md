## 🎯 Primary Objectives:
* Confirm core requirements – Compensation, location, enterprise sales fit.
* Gauge candidate's level of interest – Are they genuinely excited about the role?
* Test for counteroffer risk & early objections – Identify potential roadblocks before presenting them to the hiring team.
* Get them engaged with the opportunity – Ensure they're invested in the process and see this as a great move.
* Secure commitment for an initial interview – Reduce drop-offs and increase hiring efficiency.

## 📞 Tech Sales Screening Call Process

### Step 1️⃣: Opener – Set the Tone (1 min)
#### Goal: 
Make the candidate feel hand-picked and establish credibility.

#### Script:
"Hi {{Candidate Name}}, this is {{Your Name}} with {{Recruiting Agency/Company}}. Thanks for taking the time—I know you're busy, so I'll keep this quick."

"I came across your background and was really impressed. You've got an outstanding track record, and I wanted to reach out because I'm working with a high-growth company that needs someone exactly like you."

"Before I present you to their leadership team, I'd love to understand where you are in your career right now and what you're looking for next. Sound good?"

#### ✅ Why? 
Frames the conversation as high-value and makes them feel chosen.

### Step 2️⃣: Confirm Job Search Status (2-3 min)
#### Goal: 
Determine if they are actively looking or passively open, so you can position the opportunity accordingly.

#### Script:
"Before I dive into the role, I want to make sure this aligns with what you're looking for. Where are you at in your job search? Are you actively looking, or just open to the right opportunity?"

* If actively looking:
  * "Great—what's most important to you in your next role?"
* If passive but open:
  * "Got it—so what kind of opportunity would make you consider a move?"

#### ✅ Why? 
Helps you tailor how you pitch the role based on their motivations.

### Step 3️⃣: Validate Core Requirements (3-4 min)
#### Goal: 
Confirm alignment on compensation, location, and enterprise sales fit.

#### Script:
"I want to make sure this is a strong mutual fit, so let's cover a few quick details."

* Compensation: "What's your current base + OTE, and what range are you targeting for your next role?"
* Location & Remote Work: "The role is {{Remote/In-Office/Hybrid}}—does that work for you?"
* Enterprise Sales Fit: "What's the typical deal size and sales cycle you're used to?"

#### ✅ Ideal Answers for an Enterprise AE Role:
* $80K-$100K base, $150K+ OTE
* $50K-$250K ACV, 3-9 month sales cycles

#### ❌ Red Flags: 
SMB focus, short-cycle transactional sales (<30 days).

#### ✅ Why? 
Ensures alignment upfront and avoids wasting time.

### Step 4️⃣: Gauge Interest & Sell the Role (3 min)
#### Goal: 
Ensure the candidate is genuinely excited about the role.

#### Script:
"So, a bit about the company—{{Company Name}} is a leader in {{Industry}} with {{Number of Clients}} clients, including {{Key Client Examples}}."

"They specialize in {{Key Differentiator—e.g., enterprise-focused outbound selling, SaaS deal cycles, AI-driven solutions, etc.}}."

"The role is all about {{Key Selling Points—e.g., strategic outbound selling, high-value deals, long sales cycles}}. Based on what you've told me, this sounds like a strong fit."

"On a scale of 1-10, how excited are you about this opportunity?"

* If 8-10: "Awesome! What's getting you most excited?"
* If 6-7: "What would need to change for this to be a 10?"
* If 5 or below: "It sounds like this might not be the right fit—what's holding you back?"

#### ✅ Why? 
Quickly surfaces energy levels and potential objections early.

### Step 5️⃣: Quick Sales Validation (3-4 min)
#### Goal: 
Test their outbound sales strategy, enterprise deal cycle knowledge, and urgency-building skills.

#### Script:
"Just so I can present you accurately, I have a couple of quick questions about your sales process."

* Outbound Strategy Fit: "How do you structure an outbound sales campaign to break into new enterprise accounts?"
* Complex Sales Cycle Experience: "Tell me about a complex, multi-stakeholder deal you closed—what were the biggest challenges?"
* Driving Urgency in a Long Sales Cycle: "How do you move deals forward when there's no clear urgency?"

#### ✅ Why? 
Ensures they have the right sales DNA for the role.

### Step 6️⃣: Counteroffer Risk Check (2 min)
#### Goal: 
Reduce the risk of candidate drop-off due to counteroffers.

#### Script:
"If {{Company Name}} extended an offer at your target compensation, would you accept?"

* If YES: "Great! What would make this a total no-brainer for you?"
* If MAYBE: "What are the biggest factors you'd need to consider?"
* If NO: "What's holding you back from saying yes?"

#### ✅ Why? 
Prevents late-stage surprises that could derail the placement.

### Step 7️⃣: Close Strong & Lock in Next Steps (2 min)
#### Goal: 
Secure verbal commitment for an interview.

#### Script:
"{{Candidate Name}}, this sounds like a strong match. I'm meeting with {{Hiring Manager Name}} tomorrow to present top candidates."

"If they're interested, would you be open to an initial conversation with them?"

* If YES:
  * "Great! I'll pitch you tomorrow and follow up right after with next steps. In the meantime, do you have any questions for me?"
* If NO or Hesitant:
  * "I totally get it—what would you need to see or know for this to feel like the right next step for you?"

#### ✅ Why? 
Locks in commitment and prevents drop-offs.
